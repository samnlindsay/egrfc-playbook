## Attack principles

{: .blue-title .fs-4 .lh-tight}
> **Picture**
>
> Can we change the picture of what we're seeing in front of us?
> After 5 phases, can we do something different to create pressure on the opposition?


{: .red-title .fs-4 .lh-tight}
> **Pressure**
>
> Can we relieve pressure and put it back on the opposition?
> After 3 phases, can we do something different to create pressure on the opposition?

{: .green-title .fs-4 .lh-tight}
> **Scraps**
>
> Playing off the scraps means we embrace the hard yards.
> We hunt down every loose ball, dominate broken play, and create game-changing opportunities from absolute chaos!

## 1-3-3-1 structure

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/attack/1-3-3-1.png' | relative_url }}" style="width: 100%; height: auto;"/>

In open play, we structure our game around a 1-3-3-1 pattern of forwards, with two "pods" covering the middle of the pitch and two "edge forwards" in the 15m channels as a potential ball carrier for the backs playing to an edge.

In the midfield, this gives us a solid structure with organised attacking options on both sides.
